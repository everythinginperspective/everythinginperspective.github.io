## The Mnemonic

English has several **silent letter combinations** that trip up learners and spellers:

**Major silent letter patterns:**
- **KN-**: "Know your **kn**ights" (knee, know, knife, knot)
- **WR-**: "**Wr**ite with care" (write, wrist, wrap, wreck, wrong)
- **PS-**: "**Ps**ychology is **p**sychological" (psychology, psalm, pseudo)
- **GH-**: "**Gh**ost in the machine" (ghost, ghetto)
- **MN-**: "**Mn**emonic itself!" (mnemonic, pneumonia)
- **-MB**: "Du**mb** and nu**mb**" (dumb, numb, limb, bomb, tomb)
- **-GHT**: "Tou**ght** fi**ght**" (tough, fight, night, right, light)

## How It Works

These silent letters are **historical remnants** from older English pronunciations or borrowed from other languages:

- **KN-**: Medieval English pronounced the K: "kn-eye" → "ni"
- **WR-**: Old English pronounced the W: "wr-ite" → "rite"
- **PS-**: Greek origin: "ps-ycle" → "sycle" (psychology from Greek "psyche")
- **-MB**: Germanic: "nu-mb" → "numb" (the M was once pronounced)

## Memory Technique

**Create vivid associations:**

1. **KN = Knights in shining armor**: Know, knight, knee, knife, knack, knead
   - Picture medieval knights to lock in the K sound is silent

2. **WR = Writer's wrist**: Write, wrist, wrap, wreck, wrong, wren, wreath
   - Visualize a writer's wrist moving across paper (no W sound)

3. **PS = Psychology professor**: Psychology, psalm, pseudo, psychiatry, psyche
   - Think of a quirky psychology professor (Greeks studying the mind)

4. **-MB = Dumb and Numb**: Dumb, numb, limb, bomb, tomb, womb, climb, dumb
   - The M is completely silent in English (used to be pronounced)

5. **GH = Ghost** (sometimes silent, sometimes pronounced like F):
   - Silent: ghost, ghetto, ghastly
   - Like F: tough, laugh, cough, rough

## Common Patterns

| Silent Letters | Words | Memory |
|---|---|---|
| KN- | knee, know, knight | **Kn**ow your **kn**ights |
| WR- | write, wrist, wrap | **Wr**ite with care |
| PS- | psychology, psalm | **Ps**ychology class |
| -MB | dumb, numb, limb | Du**mb** = **mb** silent |
| GH | ghost, ghetto | **Gh**ost (sometimes silent) |
| MN- | mnemonic, pneumonia | **Mn**emonic = **mn** silent |

## Advanced Silent Letters

- **H** after other letters: honest, hour, heir (the H is silent at the start)
- **U** after Q: queue, quit, quilt (the U is often silent or unsounded)
- **E** at the end: make, love, house (final E modifies vowels, isn't pronounced)
- **T** in some words: castle, listen, fasten, wrestle

## Practice Technique

1. Write the word with the silent letter **highlighted**
2. Say the word aloud **without** that letter
3. Repeat 3-5 times
4. Create a sentence using the word

Example:
- "**K**now" → "know" (silent K)
- Sentence: "You **know** what? That **knight** doesn't **know** a **knife**."

## Why This Matters

Silent letters affect:
- **Spelling**: You must memorize them (no pure phonetic spelling)
- **Pronunciation**: Understanding pronunciation helps reading
- **Etymology**: Silent letters reveal word history and origin language
