## The Mnemonic

**PEMDAS** is an acronym that tells you the correct sequence for solving mathematical expressions:

- **P**arentheses
- **E**xponents
- **M**ultiplication & **D**ivision (left to right)
- **A**ddition & **S**ubtraction (left to right)

## How It Works

When you encounter a complex expression like `2 + 3 × 4², you must:

1. Evaluate exponents first: `4² = 16`
2. Then multiply: `3 × 16 = 48`
3. Finally add: `2 + 48 = 50`

## Memory Aid

Think of PEMDAS as a **strict hierarchy of operations**. Each level must be completed before moving to the next, reading left to right at each level.

**Alternative mnemonic**: "Please Excuse My Dear Aunt Sally" (the phrase version of PEMDAS).

## Common Mistakes

- Forgetting that multiplication and division have **equal priority**—do whichever comes first (left to right)
- Same for addition and subtraction—they're **equal priority**, not A before S
