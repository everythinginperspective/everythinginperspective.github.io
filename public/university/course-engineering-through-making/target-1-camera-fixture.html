    Chapter 1 Target 1: Multi-Camera Podcasting Fixture

    You’re starting a podcast. You want multiple camera angles—wide shot, medium, close-ups. You want to use GoPro cameras for their rugged mounting, and Sony cameras for their superior color and audio. But every time you set up, you spend 30 minutes positioning each camera, checking angles, adjusting height.
    🔗
    By the end of this target, you will build a fixture that solves this problem. You will:
    🔗
    • Design a rigid frame that holds 7-10 cameras simultaneously
      🔗
    • Create adjustable, lockable mounting arms so each camera can be positioned and then stay in that exact position
      🔗
    • Build hot-swap quick-release mounts so cameras can be removed and reinserted in the identical spot
      🔗
    • Understand the engineering principles that make this work: rigidity, tolerances, load distribution, mechanical advantage
      🔗
    • Learn the materials science of why certain materials work better than others
      🔗
    • Master the manufacturing methods to build each component
      🔗
    When you’re done, setup takes 60 seconds. Every camera snaps into the same position. Your podcast starts on time.
    🔗

    What You’ve Learned.

    You’ve built a professional, multi-camera podcasting fixture. But more importantly, you’ve learned the entire engineering process:
    🔗
    1. Define requirements. What does the product need to do?
      🔗
    2. Understand constraints. What trade-offs must we make?
      🔗
    3. Choose materials wisely. Why does material choice matter?
      🔗
    4. Learn manufacturing. How do we actually build it?
      🔗
    5. Build methodically. Follow a plan, phase by phase.
      🔗
    6. Test rigorously. Verify it actually works as intended.
      🔗
    7. Think about scale. How do we grow from 1 to many?
      🔗
    Every product you see in the world—phones, cars, furniture, equipment—went through these same steps. The next target (the scoliosis support mattress) will reinforce these principles in a completely different domain.
    🔗
    You are now capable of engineering.
