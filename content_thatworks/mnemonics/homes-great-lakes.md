## The Mnemonic

**HOMES** is an acronym for the five Great Lakes of North America:

- **H**uron
- **O**ntario
- **M**ichigan
- **E**rie
- **S**uperior

## How It Works

These five freshwater lakes form a massive system spanning the US-Canada border. The acronym lets you remember all five in order (though not necessarily geographically left-to-right).

## Memory Aid

**HOMES** is itself a word—easy to visualize and recall. Imagine the five lakes as five houses in a neighborhood.

## Geographic Context

- **Lake Michigan**: Entirely within the US
- **Lake Superior**: Largest by surface area
- **Lake Erie**: Shallowest
- **Lake Huron**: Second largest
- **Lake Ontario**: Smallest

## Fun Fact

These lakes contain about 21% of the world's fresh surface water and are critical to North American ecology and commerce.
